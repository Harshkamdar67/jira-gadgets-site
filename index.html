Small gadgets for Jira dashboards

Each app does one job well, runs entirely on Atlassian's platform, and never sends your data anywhere else.

Dashboard Notes

Formatted notes, checklists, tables, links and images on any Jira dashboard. Follows your light or dark theme. Free.

Epic Progress

Progress bars for every epic by issues, story points or time, with completion forecasts and at-risk, overdue, stalled and scope-growing flags.

How these apps handle your data

  • Built on Atlassian Forge and hosted by Atlassian. There are no servers of ours.
  • What you enter stays in Atlassian's app storage for your site.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies.
